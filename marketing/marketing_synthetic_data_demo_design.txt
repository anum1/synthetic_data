**Marketing synthetic dataset**,For a strong dashboard demo, you want to model the **entire marketing funnel**, 
connect spend to pipeline/revenue, and deliberately plant a few business stories that AI can discover.

demo story:

> **“Which marketing investments are actually driving revenue, where are we wasting spend, and what should we do next?”**

## 1. The marketing funnel

Build the synthetic data around this flow:
```text
Marketing Spend
      ↓
Campaigns / Channels
      ↓
Impressions / Clicks / Engagement
      ↓
Leads
      ↓
MQL
      ↓
SQL
      ↓
Opportunity
      ↓
Won Deal
      ↓
Revenue
```

Then add **customer, product, geography and time dimensions** so you can slice the entire funnel.

# 2. Core tables I recommend

I'd create around **12–15 tables**.

### A. Marketing master data

| Table           | Purpose                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `dim_campaign`  | Campaign master                            |
| `dim_channel`   | Paid Search, LinkedIn, Email, Events, etc. |
| `dim_customer`  | Prospects/customers                        |
| `dim_product`   | Products / product families                |
| `dim_sales_rep` | Sales ownership                            |
| `dim_geography` | Country → State → City → Region            |
| `dim_date`      | Calendar/time intelligence                 |

### B. Marketing activity

| Table                 | Purpose                    |
| --------------------- | -------------------------- |
| `fact_campaign_daily` | Daily campaign performance |
| `fact_ad_performance` | Ad-level performance       |
| `fact_email_activity` | Email sends/opens/clicks   |
| `fact_web_activity`   | Website sessions/events    |
| `fact_lead`           | Lead lifecycle             |
| `fact_lead_activity`  | Touchpoints/interactions   |

### C. Revenue funnel

| Table                       | Purpose                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `fact_opportunity`          | Sales opportunities                         |
| `fact_opportunity_stage`    | Stage progression                           |
| `fact_campaign_attribution` | Marketing → opportunity/revenue attribution |

This lets you demonstrate **marketing → sales → revenue**, which is much more compelling than a standard digital marketing dashboard.

Don't generate completely random data.

Create **intentional scenarios** that your dashboard/AI can discover.

# 19. One particularly powerful demo story

I'd structure the actual demo like this:

### Step 1 — CMO opens dashboard

> **Marketing generated $38.7M revenue from $18.4M spend.**

Looks good.

### Step 2 — AI asks:

> “Is our marketing actually becoming more efficient?”

AI discovers:

```text
Spend       +24%
Leads       +31%
MQL         +18%
Pipeline     +8%
Revenue      +3%
```

**Problem:** spend is growing much faster than revenue.

---

### Step 3 — Drill into channels

AI finds:

```text
LinkedIn
Spend       +48%
Revenue      +6%

Webinars
Spend       +12%
Revenue     +41%
```

Now you have an insight:

> **Marketing is over-investing in LinkedIn while under-investing in webinars.**

---

### Step 4 — Drill into funnel

LinkedIn:

```text
Lead → MQL       35%
MQL → SQL        11%
SQL → Won        18%
```

Webinar:

```text
Lead → MQL       48%
MQL → SQL        34%
SQL → Won        42%
```

Now you know **why**.

---

### Step 5 — Ask AI:

> “What if I move $1M from LinkedIn to webinars?”

Your synthetic dataset can have an **optimization/scenario model** that estimates:

```text
Current pipeline:       $96M

Scenario pipeline:     $103M

Incremental pipeline:   +$7M
```

That's a much stronger demo than:

> “Here is a marketing dashboard.”

It's a complete **Marketing Performance → Root Cause → Recommendation → What-if** story.

## My recommendation

Don't make this just a **digital marketing dataset**.

Make it an **enterprise Marketing-to-Revenue dataset**:

**Campaign → Engagement → Lead → MQL → SQL → Opportunity → Revenue**

with **attribution, customer journey, spend, product, geography, industry and sales dimensions**.

That gives you a much richer demo and lets you show 
**descriptive analytics + drill-down + root-cause analysis + AI + attribution + what-if planning** from the same dataset.
